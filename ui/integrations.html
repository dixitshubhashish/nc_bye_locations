Similar Market Analysis Logic & Mathematical Formulation

Analytical Logic:
• Reference Population = Median population of ZIPs containing selected Subject Brand (25,000)
• Formula: population_difference_pct = ABS(candidate_population - reference_population) / reference_population
• Population Similarity Score: MAX(0, 1 - population_difference_pct)
• Candidate qualifies when: population_difference_pct <= 20%

Data Confidence Calculation Model

🛡️ HIGH CONFIDENCE
  • Recent source ingestion (0–7 freshness days)
  • ZIP completeness > 98%
  • Coordinate completeness > 95%
  • Duplicate rate < 2%
  • Low validation failure rate (< 1%)
⚠️ MEDIUM CONFIDENCE
  • Aging source (8–30 freshness days)
  • Some address/ZIP incompleteness (85–98%)
  • Centroid-fallback coordinate resolution
  • Moderate duplicate rate (2–5%)
⛔ LOW CONFIDENCE
  • Stale source (30+ freshness days)
  • High missingness in ZIP / Coordinates (< 85%)
  • High duplicate or validation failure rate (> 5%)
  • Partial or broken acquisition batch
