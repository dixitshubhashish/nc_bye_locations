How this works

1. Pick a brand and a file

Choose which brand this data belongs to, then tell us where the data is coming from — an uploaded file, a public link, an API, or a custom script. Click Parse and we'll take a quick look to see what's inside.

2. AI matches your fields automatically

You'll see your actual records on one side, and how each column lines up to a location field on the other. Green means AI matched it automatically, using this and every past mapping you've confirmed; light red means it's a guess worth double-checking.

3. Save, then review

Saving processes your entire file, not just the quick preview. Good records go straight into your reporting. Anything that needs a second look shows up in Review Error Listings — nothing is ever silently dropped.

4. AI cleans up and enriches your data

In the background, AI repairs common problems automatically: ZIP codes and state names normalized, swapped or inverted coordinates fixed, offshore/wrong coordinates snapped to the nearest real city, and likely-duplicate brand entries flagged for a safe merge. A value that plainly can't be real for its field — a coordinate no place on Earth actually has, a date that isn't a date — is cleared rather than kept, so the system can fill in a real one instead of storing junk. Anything it can't confidently fix lands in Review with a suggested fix already attached — accept it, or fix it by hand and retry.

5. Where things live

  • Mappings: bring in your data, match its fields, and manage brands.
  • Review Error Listings: find, fix, and retry anything that didn't save cleanly.
  • Template Library: reuse a mapping you've already set up, instead of starting over.
  • Reporting: see how brands compare, spot coverage gaps, and check data quality.

6. About the numbers you'll see

Reporting shows the latest data we have. It updates quietly in the background, and you can always hit refresh for the newest numbers. Filters only affect the report you're currently looking at. In Data Quality, the breakdown table can be viewed by Brand, City, State, or Country — switch the dropdown above the table to see the same invalid / needs-review / AI-fixed counts sliced whichever way is useful.
